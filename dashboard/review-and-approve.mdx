Review is where mistakes get caught, because signing is where the batch stops being editable. Nothing after this step can change what gets paid.

## Review the batch

On the batch detail page, check the payout as it will actually execute:

- The recipient list, with each amount and claim date
- The **total payout**
- The rail and payment type
- The payment date, for scheduled payouts

Fix anything now — amounts and claim dates are editable in place until you sign.

## Sign the batch

Select **Sign Batch**. The **Sign to Confirm Payout** dialog summarizes the rail, recipient count, total amount, and scheduled claim date — compare these against what you intended, then select **Sign Record**.

<Note>
  Signing makes the payout tamper-proof, but it does not initiate the payout. After you sign, the recipients, amounts, and other details cannot be changed — even by Pvium.
</Note>

Signing is cryptographic finalization: your signature commits to the exact payment set, and altering any detail afterward invalidates it. The payout that gets funded is exactly the payout you reviewed. See [Security](/concepts/security#cryptographic-payout-finalization) for how the guarantee works.

Once signed, the batch shows as finalized and is ready to fund.
