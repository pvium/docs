---
title: "Export records"
description: "Export payout records for finance, accounting, and audits"
---

To export settled payments, open the **Records** page for your organization.

1. Filter by settlement date range, payment type, or batch ID.
2. Review the matching payees and transactions.
3. Select **Export CSV** to download the records matching your filters.

Use the transaction references to reconcile payments against blockchain settlement. See [Records and exports](/controls/records-and-exports) for the available record details.
