---
title: "Roles and permissions"
description: "Control who can create, approve, fund, and manage payouts"
---

Decide who in your team prepares payouts, reviews their details, manages API keys, and authorizes funding. Give each integration only the API permissions it needs, and restrict access to private signing keys and the funding wallet.

See [Authentication](/developers/authentication) for credentials and [Access control](/concepts/security#access-control) for the permissions and signatures required by payout operations.
