---
title: "Approval workflows"
description: "Require payout review before money moves"
---

Separate payout preparation, review, and funding in your team’s process. Review recipients, amounts, and dates before signing the batch. The person responsible for your treasury wallet then funds the signed payout under your organization’s approval policy.

See [Review and sign](/dashboard/review-and-approve) for batch review and [Funding controls](/concepts/security#funding-works-like-a-checkout) for how your wallet’s approval process applies.
