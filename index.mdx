Pvium helps platforms orchestrate compliant payouts to contractors, creators, affiliates, vendors, and other global earners without taking custody of your funds.

Use these docs to learn how to send payouts without handing over your funds to any thirdparty, while Pvium handles the compliance work around them: Identity, W-8 and W-9 collection, sanction screening, audit trails, status tracking, automated payout runs, and exports for reconciliation in tools like QuickBooks.
