---
title: "Batch payouts"
description: "Understand instant, scheduled, pooled, and milestone batch payouts"
---

Batch payouts let you pay many recipients in one payout run. A batch gives finance and operations teams one place to review recipients, totals, approval status, delivery status, and records.

## Instant batches

Use an instant batch when everyone in the batch should be paid after approval and funding.

## Scheduled batches

Use a scheduled batch when everyone in the batch should be eligible for payment on or after a specific date.

## Pooled batches

Use a pooled batch when funds are set aside for a group and recipients receive funds based on eligibility or receive timing.

## Milestone batches

Use a milestone batch when payment depends on completed work, approval, or a project stage.

## Records for every batch

Each batch should leave finance with records they can reconcile:

- Recipient list
- Amounts
- Payout type
- Schedule or release condition
- Approval history
- Delivery status
- Exportable records for accounting and audit review
