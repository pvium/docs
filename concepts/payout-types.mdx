---
title: "Payout types"
description: "Choose the right payout model for your workflow"
---

Pvium supports different payout types because payout operations are not all the same. Some payments need to go out immediately. Others should unlock on a date, after a milestone, or from a shared pool.

## Instant payouts

Use an instant payout when the recipient should be paid as soon as the payout is reviewed, approved, and funded.

Instant payouts are useful for:

- One-off contractor payments
- Creator or affiliate payouts that are already approved
- Urgent vendor payments
- Small payout runs that do not need a future delivery date

## Scheduled payouts

Use a scheduled payout when recipients should receive funds on or after a specific date.

Scheduled payouts are useful for:

- Weekly contractor payouts
- Monthly affiliate payouts
- Payroll-like payout cycles
- Future-dated rewards or rebates

## Pooled payouts

Use a pooled payout when you want to fund a payout pool and let eligible recipients receive funds from that pool.

Pooled payouts are useful for:

- Large recipient groups
- Rewards programs
- Community or marketplace payout pools
- Payouts where the exact receive timing varies by recipient

## Milestone payouts

Use a milestone payout when payment should depend on a completed deliverable, approval, or project stage.

Milestone payouts are useful for:

- Contractor projects
- Agency or vendor work
- Creator campaigns
- Grants or staged disbursements
